## 1. Roswell 1947 档案片风（Seedance 2.5 · 多 Stage）

*Source: [Soumya Mahato (@soumyattention)](https://x.com/soumyattention) — [Post](https://x.com/soumyattention/status/2085947512582721619)*  
*Made with Seedance 2.5 early access on OpenArt · 2026-08-08*

```text
[Generation Goal]
Generate a recovered-archival-footage style video that looks like authentic 1947 military documentation film from the Roswell desert. The central subjects are a civilian cameraman, US Army soldiers in 1947-era uniforms, and a small humanoid alien creature. The primary event: a crash site in the hills is filmed, the cameraman is ordered off the premises, the military inspects the wreckage, and a live alien is restrained on a field gurney while it screams and struggles.

The entire video must look shot on a hand-cranked 16mm newsreel camera from 1947: black-and-white film, heavy visible grain, gate weave and frame jitter, vertical scratches, dust and hair in the gate, occasional missing frames and hard jump cuts, flickering exposure, blown-out highlights in the desert sun, soft vintage lens with vignetting, slightly unsteady handheld framing. Audio sounds like a degraded optical soundtrack: muffled mono, constant hiss and crackle, voices slightly distorted, wind buffeting the microphone.

[Stage 1] 0-8s
Handheld from a ridge overlooking dry desert hills at midday. Thin black smoke rises from metallic debris. Cameraman narrates: "Something has come down in the hills outside Roswell... the Army's already here." Two soldiers with M1 helmets enter; one raises a hand at the lens and shouts: "Turn that camera off! This area is restricted — leave the premises, now!" Image jerks away; hard splice with white leader frames.

[Stage 2] 8-15s
Army documentation camera, steadier. Soldiers move through debris; one turns a curved metallic fragment with mirror sheen; another photographs with Speed Graphic flash. Covered stretcher carried to canvas tent. Hard jump cut with film scratch.

[Stage 3] 15-27s
Canvas tent, bare bulb. Small grey-skinned humanoid alien strapped to field gurney, thrashing, unearthly shriek. Soldiers pin shoulders and legs; medical coat leans in. Offhand voice: "Feisty little fella, ain't he." Push-in, jitter, occasional soft focus.

[Stage 4] 27-30s
Film degrades mid-struggle — frames slip, white leader, projector clatter and hiss.

[Maintain Consistency]
B&W 1947 16mm look, grain, scratches, flicker, degraded mono audio across all stages. 1947 US Army khaki + M1 helmets. Alien identity locked. No modern objects, no color, no clean digital look.
```

## 8. Visual Production Graph 工作流（Seedance 2.0）

*Source: [Heather Cooper (@HBCoop_)](https://x.com/HBCoop_) — [Post](https://x.com/HBCoop_/status/2050246433480020154)* · 2026-05-01

**方法：** 用一张「视觉制作图」压缩角色 + 世界 + 空间布局 + 镜头序列，再配短文本只负责 timing / camera / shot order。减少模型瞎猜，提高多镜头连贯。

```text
[Use Visual Production Graph image as primary control]
Execute shot order and camera moves only as listed:
1) Establishing wide — slow push
2) Medium character intro — eye-level
3) Detail insert — shallow DOF
4) Closing hero — gentle orbit
Keep identity, wardrobe, and location locked to the graph. Natural motion, cinematic continuity.
```
